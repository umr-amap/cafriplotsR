# Email template: notifying a data provider that their inventory is archived in CafriplotsR

Use this template when reaching out to someone who shared plot/transect data with you
in the past, to let them know it has been integrated into CafriplotsR, and to ask
whether they're comfortable with it staying there, and whether it should remain
private or become open to other users.

Fill in the bracketed placeholders before sending.

---

**Subject: [SITE/PROJECT NAME] inventory data — now archived in CafriplotsR**

Dear [NAME],

I hope this finds you well. [OPTIONAL: personal opening line / how long since last contact.]

I'm writing to let you know about a database/R package I've been developing called
**CafriplotsR**. Its purpose is to bring together forest plot and transect inventory
data from Central Africa into a shared infrastructure, while preserving data
sovereignty for each contributor: each research group keeps full control over who can
access their own data, but the underlying taxonomy, traits, and tools are shared and
standardized across all inventories. The aim is to improve the visibility of fieldwork
done across the region, make historical data (which too often gets lost or becomes
inaccessible over time) usable in the long term, and make compilations and analyses
more reproducible for everyone.

The reason I'm writing to you specifically: about [X years] ago, you kindly shared with
me the plot data from [SITE/PROJECT NAME], from the project you coordinated there
[Y years ago / around DATE]. I've kept that dataset, and I have now integrated it into
CafriplotsR. At present it is **not accessible to anyone but me** — exactly as is the
case for all inventories in the system unless the data provider chooses otherwise.

The main advantage of having it there is that it stays properly archived, linked to an
up-to-date taxonomic backbone, and reusable — for you, your collaborators, or others
working on Central African forests — rather than sitting unused on an old hard drive.

I just wanted to flag that this data is there, and to ask:
1. Are you comfortable with it remaining archived in CafriplotsR?
2. If so, would you prefer it stay **private** (visible only to me/you, as it is now),
   or would you be open to making it **openly accessible** to other users of the system?

Happy to give you more details about the package, or to set up a call if that's easier.
Let me know what you think.

Best regards,
Gilles
